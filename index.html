● Speed-X Bus & 433 MHz RF

SMK Technologies is proud to offer advanced security solutions featuring the Hikvision DS-PHA64-KIT-WB(B) AX Hybrid Pro wired intrusion alarm system.

This high-performance alarm system supports up to 64 alarm zones, including 8 onboard zones, expandable wired and wireless peripherals, and flexible communication options. It integrates with iVMS-4200 and Hik-Partner Pro software for programming, monitoring, and remote management.

Kit Peripherals & Sensor Catalog

Explore the certified components included with and supported by the AX Hybrid Pro platform.

DS-PD2-P18CE

PIR Detector

The DS-PD2-P18CE features a long, narrow detection pattern designed for shop fronts and large windows. Bluewave technology, automatic sensitivity, and digital temperature compensation help it adapt to environmental conditions.

  • 18 m digital curtain detection coverage
  • Dual-element pyro-electric sensor
  • Selectable End-of-Line (EOL) resistors
  • Automatic sensitivity adjustment
DS-PKF1-WB

Wireless Keyfob

A compact remote control for convenient operation of compatible Hikvision alarm systems. Allows users to easily arm, disarm, and trigger emergency functions with a single press.

  • Stay / away arming and disarming
  • Keyfob lock and unlock function
  • Remote configuration through mobile app
  • Frequency hopping against jamming
DS-PD1-MC-WS

Magnetic Contact

A surface-mounted magnetic door and window contact designed to detect unauthorized opening in residential and commercial environments with high durability.

  • Flame-retardant ABS protective shell
  • Normally Closed (NC) connection type
  • Designed for surface mounting on doors
  • Detection Gap ≥17 mm verified threshold
DS-PT-M1

Identification Tag

A compact identification tag for convenient access control with compatible security systems and readers. Enables quick touch authentication without memorizing passcodes.

  • Small, solid, lightweight structure
  • Convenient to carry on keychains
  • 13.56 MHz operating frequency (Mifare)
  • Durable PVC & ABS construction
DS-PK1-LRT-HWB

Wired LCD Keypad

A wired control interface designed for convenient operation and monitoring of compatible Hikvision alarm systems. Featuring a clear LCD display and Speed-X bus.

  • Backlit LCD display screen
  • One-touch arming & Emergency keys
  • Built-in RFID Tag reader
  • Supports up to 64 keyfobs & RS-485
